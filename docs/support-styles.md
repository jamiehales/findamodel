# Support styles of pre-supported models

The support rebuild (`backend/src/geometry/support_layout.rs`, `support_separation.rs`) has to read
supports made by a handful of tools, each with its own way of building a support, and by creators
who use those tools in their own way. This note groups the supported models in the catalog by the
tool that made the supports and the structure the supports form, with a fingerprint for each that
can be measured from the mesh, so check models can be grouped and results reported per group.

The style probe, the grouping script, the benchmark list and its runner named below are kept
with the support audit's working files, outside the repository.

## How the groups were found

- 596 pre-supported STLs were measured: the 154-model round-trip check corpus, 299 models sampled
  from every (creator, exporter) group in the instance, and 143 more from the creators added since
  (One Page Rules, PiperMakes, Factory Fortress, The Witchguild, Quentin Miniatures, and others).
  The instance holds about 45,000 supported models (about 31,000 STLs) from 43 creators.
- A style probe splits off the supports as the app does and records: connected shells and their
  shapes (rod, cone, ball), how many support triangles are congruent to many others (generated
  primitives repeat one triangle shape), the corner count of the round outlines in horizontal
  slices (the shaft polygon), the polygon of the slice through each tip's middle and of the slices
  just above the plate, the plate footprint rasterised at 0.2 mm (filled share of its convex hull,
  share more than 0.6 mm from an edge, pieces), and counts from the rebuilt layout (trunks,
  branches, struts, braces and their angles, X crossings, zigzag chains, kickstands, fans of tips
  meeting in one hub, tip length and widths).
- Renders of the original supports (whole model, plate end, plate from above, one tip) were read
  for every creator, and for every model where the measured group and the creator's house style
  disagreed.
- STL headers (`CHITUBOX_Engine_export`, `Generated by Formware 3D`, `MESHMIXER`, Materialise's
  `COLOR= MATERIAL=`) and folder names were used only to confirm a tool, never as a feature. A
  header records the last program that wrote the file, which is often not the one that made the
  supports: Lychee supports re-exported through Chitubox, netfabb, Magics, Meshmixer or Blender
  keep every Lychee fingerprint.

## The tool: polygon counts

Every support tool in the catalog builds supports from low-polygon primitives (cylinders, cones,
spheres) and writes them as separate, overlapping shells; none unions them into one mesh. The
polygon counts of three parts tell the tools apart without exception in the sample:

| Tool | Shaft | Tip cone | Root | Other marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chitubox | 16 (some exports 8) | 16 | 16 (4 on a raft of square tiles) | joint balls as separate spheres, 3 to 6 ball shells per tip; tip cone 1.6 to 2.0 mm |
| Lychee | 8 | 8 | 8 (4 on a raft of square tiles) | under 1 ball shell per tip; tip cone about 2.9 to 3.9 mm |
| Formware | 32 | 32 | 32 | raft with round drain holes and a raised rim |
| 12-gon pillars | 12 | 12 | 12 | one polygon pad following the footprint's hull, independent pillars, no braces |
| DragonFruit (STL export, from its source) | 8 | 16 | 32 (disc plus cone) | 16 x 16 joint spheres; blank header |

Almost every support triangle is congruent to many others (median 96 to 100% of support
triangles share their edge lengths with at least 9 others). Geometry classed as supports with
few repeated triangles and no common shaft polygon is a sculpted part of an unsupported model.
(A large custom raft can bring a real support mesh under 60% repeated triangles, as on
BattleCat's bullet casing cfb6db2c, but its shafts still share one polygon.)

The 12-gon pillars look like PrusaSlicer's SLA supports, but nothing confirms the tool (the files
carry an 80-byte header of the bytes 0 to 79). Only Aradia Miniatures uses them.

## The structure

What the supports form, read from the rebuilt layout:

- Trees and plain posts: trunks standing on the plate, with branches hanging from them and a few
  braces. Plain unbraced posts are the same structure with no branches.
- X-braced lattice: posts tied to their neighbours by braces, many of them crossing in an X
  between the same two posts. In Lychee models the lattice usually forms a cage of posts around
  the model, tied by X or single diagonal braces, with trees inside it.
- Zigzag web: posts braced to their neighbours by diagonals that chain end to end up the pair
  (one brace starts where the last one ended), with few trees.
- Hollow struts: struts running from one face of a hollowed model to another, inside it, tied by
  rungs, with nothing on the plate.
- Independent pillars: straight pillars, each on its own, with no braces or trees.

### Raft is an attribute, not a group

Creators switch raft types freely, and the raft does not change how the supports above it are
rebuilt (the raft stays out of the rebuild; DragonFruit builds its own from its raft setting). The
raft is recorded per model as an attribute only:

- raft: a solid raft of overlapping square tiles, merged discs, or one slab with a rim.
- web: a triangulated truss of bars on the plate, often with a ball at every node (Lychee's web,
  Eastman's line raft); the plate hull is under 70% solid.
- pads: no raft; every trunk stands on its own flared root (under half the hull filled).
- pad: one polygon pad under independent pillars.
- none: no plate footprint (hollow struts).

The Lychee cage and the web raft usually come together (89 of the 104 lattice models stand on a
web), but neither defines the other: 49 Lychee tree models stand on a web and 15 lattice models
on a solid raft.

### chitubox-trees

Chitubox's 16-gon supports (and Formware's 32-gon ones) as trees and plain posts. Tip cones are
separate shells (BS Sculpt, BattleCat) or welded to a contact ball; joint balls are separate
spheres (3 per tip); tip cone 1.7 mm long, contact 0.43 mm, shaft 1.2 mm. Few braces (0.5 per
trunk, mostly 45 degree diagonals), few X crossings, no kickstands; 15% of tips are branches. ARCS
terrain stands on pads (a flared root per trunk) with long thin trunks and level rungs. Formware's
7 models (Cast n Play bases and terrain, one Loot model) are trees of long slender tips with many
ball shells, as Chitubox builds them, and are kept here rather than as a group of their own.
Examples: 630a133f, 570d996b (BS Sculpt), 70b02245, e8be9538 (Adament Arsenal), f6408e9f
(BattleCat), 88319e5e (ARCS, pads), 06b72042 (Formware).

### chitubox-xlattice

Chitubox supports tied into a lattice by X braces: 39% of braces cross another between the same
two supports, 1.7 braces per trunk. Thin shafts (0.62 mm) and contacts, many supports per model
(median 178). Dakka Dakka and Dakka Terrain walls and terrain panels, some Loot Studios, Bite the
Bullet and Under the Odd Willow models.
Examples: fcf1d055 (Dakka Dakka), 43569c7d (Dakka Terrain), 8e3a9612 (Bite the Bullet's
Commander floor), 1aec4e59 (Loot).

### lychee-trees

Lychee's 8-gon supports as free-standing trees and posts: long tip cones (2.9 mm, contact
0.38 mm), shafts 1.15 mm, 41% of tips are branches, under one brace per trunk and few X
crossings, some kickstands. On a solid raft (Loot Studios, Kinsfolk, GoonMaster, Malediction) or a
web (small Creature Caster, Claymore and Factory Fortress models). Bite the Bullet's hub fans
(tip cones meeting in one joint ball) are here.
Examples: 03587c94 (Loot), f03f9e3a (GoonMaster), 70f1bf40 (Kinsfolk), 64e857fb (Bite the
Bullet's Boss Dragon Tail), b297fce9 (Creature Caster, on a web).

### lychee-lattice

Lychee posts braced into a cage around the model (X braces or single diagonals between
neighbouring posts, 2.2 braces per trunk), with trees inside it (63% of tips are branches) and
kickstands (0.6 per trunk). Most stand on a web raft whose bars form the cage's floor; Under the
Odd Willow, h3LL Creator and some One Page Rules and Kinsfolk models build the same cage on a
solid raft. Fans of tips into one hub are most common here.
Examples: 203d8125 (Claymore), 56ea9e5a (Heroes Infinite), e792cdab (Flesh of Gods), f92c8bc5
(Bite the Bullet's Cultist Leader, on a raft), 027a8dc7 (Under the Odd Willow, on discs).

### lychee-zigzag

Lychee posts, each braced to its neighbours by zigzag diagonals that chain end to end (38% of
braces share an end with the next one on the same pair), with few trees (9% branches), 2 braces
per trunk and many supports. Eastman's busts and bases stand on a fine line raft (web), with many
shells per tip (posts, braces and web bars are separate primitives) and tips often fanned from a
post's top; PiperMakes and The Witchguild follow the same style. Loot Studios, Malediction and
Station Forge build zigzag-braced posts on a solid raft.
Examples: 0293692c, 2c8acb53, d205eca4 (Eastman busts), 024a5729 (PiperMakes), 0b821d18 (The
Witchguild), 07f6f4b2 (Malediction, on a raft).

### hollow-struts

Death x Tiles' hollowed terrain tiles, supported inside: Lychee 8-gon struts run from one face of
the hollow to another (98% of supports are struts), often in pairs meeting at one contact, tied
by rungs. No plate footprint (the tile itself stands on the plate); long tips (3.9 mm). Death x
Tiles' solid parts (an arm, 363c280a) are Lychee lattices.
Examples: 049c53f2, 0e4bfe5b, 87abf87c, b922d78a.

### pad12-pillars

12-gon pillars, tips and roots, all standing straight on one polygon pad that follows the hull of
the footprint, with a sloped edge. No braces, no trees, no joint balls; short tips (1.5 mm) with
small contacts (0.26 mm). Eiren's Wednesday (b0f32f5b) is the exception that leans legs against
its posts. Aradia Miniatures only.
Examples: b0f32f5b, e182fbd2, 0b5e57ea.

### unsupported

Models marked supported whose "support" geometry is not supports: unsupported versions in the
same folders (Mezgike's `UNSUPP_`, One Page Rules' loose parts, Dakka Dakka's `(unsupported)`),
bases, magnet pegs and text, and Engin3d's mis-marked hard-surface kits. Fingerprint: no round
shaft outlines, or under 60% repeated triangles. A check group: the rebuild must keep none of
them.

### Flag: fans

Tips fanned from one hub (three or more branch knots within 1 mm) occur in every Lychee group and
a few Chitubox models. A model is flagged when at least 30% of its tips hang from such hubs (35
models: lychee-trees 15, lychee-lattice 12, chitubox-trees 3, lychee-zigzag 3, chitubox-xlattice
2). Bite the Bullet's hub-style models (Cultist Leader, Boss Dragon Tail) and Kinsfolk's are the
clearest.

## Which rebuild steps each group leans on

- chitubox-trees: tip cones measured from their own shell; joint balls left out of the shaft's
  line; branches walked into their trunk; pads mean each root is the trunk's own (no raft).
- chitubox-xlattice: braces from the untraced pieces between supports, X junctions split into two
  braces; thin shafts passing close to each other kept apart by their slices.
- lychee-trees: branch walks (the most vertical way at each fork), fanned tip cones as separate
  supports meeting in one hub, kickstands from legs splaying off trunks.
- lychee-lattice: braces and X junctions, posts with no contact of their own (cage posts that
  legs lean against), kickstands, and leaning shafts followed past the posts they pass.
- lychee-zigzag: chained braces between neighbouring posts (the lowest one, ending at or below
  the root top, is dropped), fans of tips from post tops, posts without a tip of their own.
- hollow-struts: strut walks ending in the model, pairs of struts meeting at one contact, the
  walk keeping to its post's line through rungs, strut heads and feet measured from their cones.
- pad12-pillars: plain trunks; Eiren's Wednesday also needs posts with legs leaning against them.
- unsupported: the separation's confidence checks and the coverage and holding guard.

## Tagging a model

The grouping script reads the style probe's rows and the first pass's reviewed tags, and applies:

1. Tool from the polygon counts above. Fewer than 4 round outlines in the slices, or under 60%
   repeated triangles with no one polygon shared by 60% of the outlines, is unsupported.
2. 60% or more of the supports struts (both ends on the model) is hollow-struts.
3. Chitubox and Formware: X crossings on 20% of braces with at least one brace per trunk is
   chitubox-xlattice; otherwise chitubox-trees.
4. Lychee: under 20% branches and 15% of braces chained end to end is lychee-zigzag; at least one
   brace per trunk and either X crossings on 20% of braces or a web under it (under 70% of the
   plate hull more than 0.6 mm from an edge) is lychee-lattice; otherwise lychee-trees.
5. 12-gon tools are pad12-pillars.

The web in rule 4 stands in for the cage: a Lychee web raft and its cage come from the same
setting, and the X-crossing count alone misses cages braced with single diagonals. Models where
the measured group and the eye disagreed keep the decision made from the renders (Eastman-style
webs whose posts carry few braces stay lychee-zigzag; a few Chitubox X lattices where the rebuild
finds too few crossings stay chitubox-xlattice).

Compared with the first pass (which split by raft type: chitubox-raft, chitubox-pads, lychee-raft,
lychee-web, web-zigzag, formware), 84 models change structure group: 48 Lychee models on a web
are free-standing trees, 16 on a solid raft are lattices, and 20 on a solid raft or web are
zigzag-braced posts. Chitubox's pads (9) and Formware (7) merge into chitubox-trees.

- Weakest surface: chitubox-xlattice (recall 0.957 at 0.3 mm, 0.871 at 0.1 mm), across all its
  creators: surface goes missing in the X lattice between thin shafts.
- lychee-zigzag rebuilds its surface about as well as the tree groups, but DragonFruit's tips
  match only about half of the original contacts (tips fanned from post tops, zigzag ends).
- lychee-lattice has the most joints per support (0.40): leaning shafts bent around cage posts.
- The one real model rejected is Factory Fortress afd0d1d3 (lychee-trees, on a web). It is a
  plate of three gun parts: the separation takes the most finely meshed part (329,000 triangles)
  as the model, and the other two, meshed far more coarsely (24,000 and 12,000 triangles over a
  similar surface), fall under both the share of the seed's triangles and the share of its
  triangle density that a model part needs, so they are classed as supports with their own
  supports. The rebuild then covers 49.5% of that "support" surface and the coverage guard
  rejects the model, as it should for what it is given. The fault is in the classification.
- The rebuild keeps none of the unsupported models, and DragonFruit dropped no entity in any group.

## Benchmark

`benchmark.tsv` holds 67 models: 9 per support group (pad12-pillars 7, the ones fast enough of
its 9) and 6 unsupported, from 29 creators, 47 of them in the check corpus. It has the round-trip
corpus columns (id, creator, set, path; set is the group) plus a style column. Each group starts
from the models earlier work named (Eiren's Wednesday; the Eastman busts Magneto Head5,
Spider-Man and Batman Beyond; Death x Tiles' FST_032 Hollow A, Wall Barrels and Mineral Wall;
Bite the Bullet's Cultist Leader and Boss Dragon Tail hubs and the Commander floor; Claymore;
Adament Arsenal 70b02245; BS Sculpt 570d996b; Factory Fortress afd0d1d3; one Formware model) and
is filled by farthest-point sampling over normalised features (plate, bracing, trees, fans, tip
shape, size), so rafts and creators mix, with at most 3 models per creator where a group has
several, files under 30 MB and none slower than 15 s.

The runner builds a worktree, runs the round trip over the benchmark and prints each group's
kept count, recall and precision at 0.1 and 0.3 mm, tips matched and joints per support against
main, and lists models worse by more than 0.02 recall at 0.3 mm. It takes about 100 s with 4
jobs. On main (ca94db7):

style probe and grouped the same way, to check that the groups hold beyond the measured models.

## Not found

None of the 596 models, nor the headers of all 32,000 supported STLs scanned, showed these:

- DragonFruit's own STL export (8-gon shafts with 16-gon tip cones and 32-gon roots). The two
  TableFlip Foundry "Cones of Calibration with raft" files are calibration prints, not supports.
- Meshmixer's tree supports (one unioned mesh of rounded struts and balls). The 14 files with a
  Meshmixer header carry Chitubox (TreadHead Rad) or Lychee (Eastman) supports re-exported.
- PrusaSlicer's organic supports (smooth, branching, unioned).
- Hand-sculpted supports (ZBrush or Blender): every real support mesh is built from repeated
  primitives. Files with a Blender header (One Page Rules, PiperMakes, Voy Forges) carry Lychee
  supports or none.
