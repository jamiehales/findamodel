* Model parts meshed much coarser than the main part are classed as support (`backend/src/geometry/support_separation.rs`). Factory Fortress' afd0d1d3 is a plate of three gun parts: the most finely meshed gun (329k triangles) becomes the model, and the other two (24k and 12k triangles over a similar surface) fall below both the "1/8 of the seed's triangles" rule (`MODEL_SEED_DIVISOR`) and the "1/4 of its triangle density" rule (`MODEL_DENSITY_DIVISOR`), so they are classed as supports with their own supports. The rebuild then covers only 49.5% of that support surface and the guard rejects the model. A possible fix is to accept large sculpted parts (few repeated triangles, unlike generated support primitives) as model parts whatever their density relative to the seed; it needs a detection version bump

* Audit the support rebuild's heuristics (`backend/src/geometry/support_layout.rs`, `support_separation.rs`). There are about 100 tuned numeric values (most of the constants in support_layout.rs and support_separation.rs; a few are limits or DragonFruit's fixed dimensions), each set while looking at a few named models. Support geometry comes from a limited number of pre-supporting tools (Chitubox, Lychee, DragonFruit, Formware, Meshmixer, hand-sculpted), so: tag the check models by the style that made their supports and report results per style; nudge each constant up and down (e.g. 25%) and rerun the round trip and check_probe to find the dead, load-bearing and fragile ones; merge near-duplicates (the "wider than the shaft" ratios, the "same point" distances) into shared named concepts; document each remaining heuristic (the shape it handles, the styles relying on it, the data behind its value) and pin it with a test; and keep a fixed style-balanced benchmark set plus a fresh random sample from the instance for each run. The bend tolerance, the tip's plateau test and the brace knot reach now scale with the measured shaft, tip and brace width; the walk's heights (SKIP_RESET_MM, PICKUP_RUN_MM, FORK_LOOKAHEAD_MM), MIN_TIP_LENGTH_MM and LOCAL_WIDTH_MM were tried as multiples of the shaft width and left fixed (Eastman's thick posts and the thin Lychee trees pulled opposite ways, or nothing moved), so a relative form for them needs a measure other than width. Prefer rules that recognise a structure where it occurs (X junctions, zigzag webs, fans) over switches on the model's style; style tags are for reporting results per style. For the load-bearing constants, sweep each style separately and compare the best values: only where one clearly differs by style and no relative measure explains it, consider a per-style value there, and only after review
