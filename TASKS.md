* Model parts meshed much coarser than the main part are classed as support (`backend/src/geometry/support_separation.rs`). Factory Fortress' afd0d1d3 is a plate of three gun parts: the most finely meshed gun (329k triangles) becomes the model, and the other two (24k and 12k triangles over a similar surface) fall below both the "1/8 of the seed's triangles" rule (`MODEL_SEED_DIVISOR`) and the "1/4 of its triangle density" rule (`MODEL_DENSITY_DIVISOR`), so they are classed as supports with their own supports. The rebuild then covers only 49.5% of that support surface and the guard rejects the model. A possible fix is to accept large sculpted parts (few repeated triangles, unlike generated support primitives) as model parts whatever their density relative to the seed; it needs a detection version bump

* Finish the open points of the support rebuild heuristics audit (`backend/src/geometry/support_layout.rs`). Each constant's doc comment gives the styles relying on it and the data behind its value; `docs/support-styles.md` has the style groups. Lychee zigzag webs (mostly Eastman) want a smaller brace end skip (`BRACE_END_SKIP` 0.1125 rather than 0.15), and no size measure explains it, so look for a rule that recognises the zigzag web rather than a switch on the style. The walk's heights (`SKIP_RESET_MM`, `PICKUP_RUN_MM`, `FORK_LOOKAHEAD_MM`), `MIN_TIP_LENGTH_MM` and `LOCAL_WIDTH_MM` need a relative measure other than the shaft width: a skip reset of 2.0 gave about 10% fewer joints, but it cut new branches and kickstands into Eastman and Mezgike models. The stricter brace coverage (`BRACE_COVERAGE` 0.9) and margin (`BRACE_MARGIN_MM` 0.075) each helped alone, but together they lost 16% of braces; try them one at a time on the full corpus. Lychee lattices would take a larger `TIP_SHARE` (0.9), partly because their tips are long, so it may want to scale with the tip's length; and the fork slope tolerance pulls two ways (Chitubox X lattices 0.075, Lychee lattices 0.3 at 17% more joints) with no measure found to explain it.
